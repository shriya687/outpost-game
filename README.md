Outpost: The Last Crew is an interactive survival game designed to help players understand the challenges of sustaining human life on Mars through NASA's scientific data.
Players take on the role of crew members at a Martian outpost, where they must complete missions and solve problems to keep the base operational. 
Through interactions and mini-games, players explore practical survival challenges, make critical decisions, and navigate trade-offs involving limited resources.
NASA's datasets and scientific research provide the foundation for these experiences, connecting real planetary science with interactive gameplay
By transforming complex space data into an engaging survival experience, Outpost aims to make planetary science more accessible 
and help players appreciate the scientific and technological challenges of future human exploration of Space.
